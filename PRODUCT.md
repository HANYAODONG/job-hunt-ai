# Product Context

## Register

Product. This is a task-oriented role intelligence and talent development system.

## Purpose

Turn changing market evidence into reviewed, versioned role definitions, then use those definitions to support explainable person-role diagnosis and learning plans.

## Users

- Role researchers and domain experts reviewing market signals and maintaining role definitions.
- Students and job seekers diagnosing skill gaps against a specific role version.
- Competition judges evaluating data quality, traceability, and end-to-end system completeness.

## Product Principles

1. Show the conclusion first, the explanation second, and raw evidence on demand.
2. Keep every diagnosis traceable to a published role version and source evidence.
3. Prefer one continuous workflow over disconnected dashboard pages.
4. Use familiar product controls and quiet visual hierarchy. Technical credibility comes from data and state, not decoration.
5. Preserve existing job-search APIs and expose future capabilities through mock-first frontend service contracts.

## Personality

Precise, calm, evidence-led, contemporary, and distinctly human-made.

## Anti-References

- Generic AI dashboards with neon gradients, glass panels, oversized metrics, and decorative particles.
- Repeated icon-card grids and nested cards.
- Dense technical metadata visible before the user asks for it.
- Charts used only to make a screen appear technical.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable on narrow screens.
